# Israeli Maintenance Source Registry (M-SOURCE, 2026-09-30)

Where official maintenance evidence for Israeli vehicles lives, what AutoKeep may do with each
source, and why.

- **Code:** `src/discovery/maintenance/registry/`. The data in `israelSources.ts` is generated by
  `tools/build-source-registry.mjs` from the committed research records in
  `docs/maintenance/data/research/`.
- **Every system with its policy:** `ISRAEL_SOURCE_REGISTRY_TABLE.md` (generated).
- **Related:** `SOURCE_ADAPTER_SPEC.md` (adapters), `ISRAEL_COVERAGE_MATRIX.md` (metrics),
  `COVERAGE_GAPS.md` (what blocks what).

## Model

- **One record per source system.** A source system is an importer's or a manufacturer's document
  system, never a vehicle model. It records:
  - manufacturers covered and vehicle kinds (a brand's car importer and its two-wheeler importer
    are different systems);
  - importer, market and origin (Israeli / global);
  - official domains and official pages;
  - source type, authority class and discovery mechanism;
  - document categories;
  - a versioned access policy;
  - owner-approval status, ownership evidence, limitations and adapter.
- **Source type** (what the source publishes) is separate from, and never converted into, the
  **evidence level** (A–E, decided per requirement and vehicle by the engine):
  - `A_DIRECT_MAINTENANCE_SCHEDULE`
  - `B_DIGITAL_MANUAL`
  - `C_STRUCTURED_WEB_MANUAL`
  - `D_RESTRICTED_OR_UNAVAILABLE`
- **Access policy:** six independent dimensions:
  - `discoveryAllowed`
  - `automatedFetchAllowed`
  - `automatedExtractionAllowed`
  - `documentCachingAllowed`
  - `structuredFactsStorageAllowed`
  - `documentRedistributionAllowed`
- **Policy values:** each dimension is `ALLOWED`, `NOT_ALLOWED`, `UNKNOWN` or
  `REQUIRES_PERMISSION`, and each value cites its evidence (kind, URL, short quote, review date,
  reviewer). Policies are versioned and append-only.
- **Validation rules** (`policyIssues`, `sourceSystemIssues`, enforced by tests):
  - `ALLOWED` needs explicit permission: a licence, written permission, terms that grant it, or a
    recorded owner decision.
  - "No terms found" never becomes `ALLOWED`.
  - robots.txt and access controls only speak to discovery and fetch, never to the legal
    dimensions.
  - `REQUIRES_PERMISSION` needs terms evidence.
- **Runtime:** each activity checks its own dimension, and only `ALLOWED` permits it:
  - discovery pages → `discoveryAllowed`;
  - downloads → `automatedFetchAllowed`;
  - reading a document → `automatedExtractionAllowed`;
  - shared knowledge → `structuredFactsStorageAllowed`.
  - AutoKeep never caches or redistributes documents: bytes are discarded after extraction.
- **Authority:** only systems whose domains the owner approved (P1, 2026-09-27) are `approved`.
  Every other system is `proposed` and is never an authority, a retrieval target or a link offered
  to users.

## What works automatically, what needs the user, what is blocked

- **Automatic today:** SYM two-wheeler manuals on sym-global.com, for models the site lists. The
  pipeline reads their "Periodical Maintenance Schedule" tables into grounded, verified
  requirements. They stay **level C** (not scheduled), because the manuals never state their
  model years.
- **User action:** every other vehicle. The app names the precise reason and offers the approved
  official page (Step 15):
  - manual access required;
  - access policy unresolved;
  - written permission required;
  - no digital source;
  - source identified but under review.
  - The user may upload the official document. It stays private, and it is authoritative only on
    an exact hash match with a known official version (Step 10).
- **Blocked by access policy:** these approved Israeli importer systems publish maintenance
  documents but may not be read automatically:
  - Toyota, Champion, Colmobil (Hyundai), Kia, Suzuki, Mayer (Honda cars, Volvo) and Ofer Avnir;
  - Samelet and Geely too, once approved as authorities.
  - This is a policy / permission gap, not an engine gap.
- **UNKNOWN:** 45 discovery, 38 fetch and 39 extraction values. UNKNOWN is honest and blocks
  automation.

**Authoritative Israeli evidence** (possible level A) can only come from the Israeli importer
systems above, once permitted. **Global manufacturer evidence** (level B at best, labelled
"not proven Israeli") comes from the global systems.

## Maintaining the registry

1. Research or update a record in `docs/maintenance/data/research/R*.json`, with evidence URLs and
   quotes.
2. Run `node tools/build-source-registry.mjs docs/maintenance/data/research src/discovery/maintenance/registry/israelSources.ts`.
3. The registry tests enforce validity:
   - ALLOWED only via an owner decision;
   - no shared CDN as a host;
   - approval only for owner-approved domains.
4. A policy change for an existing system appends a new version (`revisePolicy`); earlier
   evidence is never rewritten.
5. Approving a `proposed` system as an authority is an owner decision (P1 procedure).
