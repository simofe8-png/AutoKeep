## Takeaways

- No IL importer has a type A (direct schedule) source.
- The only IL type B source is Ofer Avnir, and it needs written permission.
- SYM global is the only manual source with no restrictive terms found. It is legally UNKNOWN (not ALLOWED) and does not resolve model years.
- KTM has the best metadata, but its terms prohibit commercial use.
